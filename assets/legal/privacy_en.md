# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Stalvi. We respect your privacy and are strongly committed to keeping your personal and financial information secure. This comprehensive Privacy Policy explains our approach to data collection, processing, and protection when you use the Stalvi mobile application (the "Application").

This Privacy Policy complies with the strict standards set forth by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Spanish Organic Law on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD 3/2018).

## 2. Data Collection and Processing
Stalvi operates exclusively under a "local-first" architecture. This means:
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** We do not collect, transmit, upload, or store your personal or financial data on any external servers, databases, or cloud services.
- **Locally Stored Data:** The data processed by the Application includes your user profile, financial transactions, budgets, account balances, and application preferences. This information is generated by you and remains strictly confined to your physical device.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Protecting your sensitive financial information is our highest priority. We employ advanced security measures to ensure your data remains inaccessible to unauthorized parties:
- **Database Encryption:** All locally stored data is heavily encrypted using SQLCipher. Your data is encrypted at rest on your device's storage.
- **Biometric and PIN Authentication:** Access to the Application is safeguarded by your device's native biometric authentication mechanisms (such as Fingerprint or FaceID) and a secure Personal Identification Number (PIN).
- **Data Isolation:** Because the Application operates offline without server synchronization, your data is inherently protected from remote server breaches, network interceptions, and third-party data mining.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We guarantee that your personal and financial data is completely private.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
- The Application does not integrate third-party analytics or tracking software that could monitor your financial habits or personal usage patterns.

## 5. Your Rights Under GDPR and LOPDGDD
As a user residing in the European Union or Spain, you are guaranteed specific rights regarding your personal data. Because Stalvi is a local-only application, you can exercise all of these rights directly within the app without needing to submit a request to us:
- **Right to Access and Portability:** You have full access to your data at all times. You can exercise your right to data portability by exporting your data as an encrypted backup file or a CSV spreadsheet at any time.
- **Right to Rectification:** You can edit, modify, or update any inaccurate or incomplete transaction, account, or budget directly within the Application's interface.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You maintain total control over your data's lifecycle. You can permanently delete specific transactions, or securely erase all Application data from your device using the "Delete All Data" function in the settings menu.
- **Right to Restriction of Processing:** Since data processing occurs entirely locally based on your direct inputs, you control when and how your data is processed by simply choosing whether or not to use the Application.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Stalvi is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 14 without the explicit consent of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors, as all data collection is strictly confined to the user's local device.

## 7. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in legal requirements or Application features. Any substantial changes will be communicated to you through an update to the Application. Your continued use of the Application following any updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
